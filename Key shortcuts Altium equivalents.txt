  Routing workflow

  1. Click the target layer in the left panel (e.g., met2 -
  69/20)
  2. Press Shift+P for the Path tool
  3. Press F3 to set path width (e.g., 0.14 µm for minimum
  met1/met2 in SKY130)
  4. Click to route, double-click or Enter to finish
  5. Use E (partial mode) to reshape existing routes by
  dragging edges/vertices
